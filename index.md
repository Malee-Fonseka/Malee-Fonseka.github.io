## How to read the write-ups

Every project page follows the same structure, so you can jump straight to what you care about:

1. **Problem** — what needed solving and why it was hard.
2. **Architecture** — the system at a glance, usually with a diagram.
3. **Key decisions** — the trade-offs I made and what I rejected.
4. **Results** — what the system achieved, with numbers where possible.
5. **What I'd change** — lessons learned with hindsight.
